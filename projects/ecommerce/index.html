Business question

How did the online retailer perform over 2025, which regions and product categories contributed most to sales, and what opportunities deserve management attention?

Approach

  1. Audited 600 order records, 90 customers and 20 products for missing IDs, duplicates and invalid references.
  2. Calculated discounted sales, cost of goods sold and gross profit.
  3. Limited recognized sales and profit to 538 completed orders, excluding 42 returns and 20 cancellations.
  4. Compared revenue across months, regions, products and categories.
  5. Documented the methodology and prepared MySQL queries for further validation.

Recommendations

  • Investigate the September revenue decline before adjusting promotional budgets.
  • Review the East region’s product mix to identify practices worth testing elsewhere.
  • Compare category margins alongside revenue before deciding which products to promote.
  • Examine returned orders by product and investigate potential causes.
